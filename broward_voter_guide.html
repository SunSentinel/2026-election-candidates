Constitutional Amendments

No. 1 Constitutional Amendment

Read Editorial Board Endorsement

Budget Stabilization Fund

Proposing an amendment to the State Constitution to increase the amount of funds that may be retained in the budget stabilization fund from 10% to 25% of general revenue collections, require the legislature to transfer the lesser of $750 million or the amount required to reach 25% of the general revenue collections each year unless certain conditions are met, and allow the legislature to withdraw funds for critical state needs.

No. 2 Constitutional Amendment

Read Editorial Board Endorsement

Exemption of Tangible Personal Property on Agricultural Land from Taxation

Proposing an amendment to the State Constitution to exempt tangible personal property habitually located or typically present on land classified as agricultural, used in the production of agricultural products or for agritourism activities, and owned by the landowner or leaseholder of the agricultural land from ad valorem taxation. If approved this amendment would first apply for tax years beginning January 1, 2027.

No. 3 Constitutional Amendment

Read Editorial Board Endorsement

Increased Homestead Exemption; Lower Cap on Increases in Non-Homestead Property Assessments

This amendment increases the homestead exemption, for all non-school taxes, to $150,000 in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028, and adjusts for inflation thereafter. It requires the Legislature to prescribe a uniform procedure for counties and municipalities, for their respective levies, to increase the homestead exemption up to full assessed value, and allows special districts, subject to referendum approval, to do the same. Persons who are not Florida residents on December 31, 2026, will receive the existing homestead exemption upon qualifying for a homestead exemption, with the increased homestead exemption beginning with the fifth year of exemption, to the extent permitted by the U.S. Constitution. This amendment reduces the annual cap on assessment increases for non-homestead properties from 10% to 5%. This amendment requires counties and municipalities to use property taxes solely for public safety, education and schools, infrastructure, natural resources, bond debt service, retirement benefits for employees, and operations and administration. Other expenditures may be approved by county officers or county or municipal governing bodies unless prohibited by general law, notwithstanding Article VII, Section 9(a) of the Florida Constitution, which allows counties and municipalities to levy property taxes for their respective purposes. This amendment takes effect January 1, 2027.

Broward County

Broward County, Question 1

Health Care Services, Such as Heart and Cancer Screenings, for County Residents, Funded by Surtax

Shall Broward County fund (1) preventive heart and cancer tests, to the extent not covered by insurance, for qualified Broward County residents, (2) primary and preventive health care for indigent or medically poor residents, and (3) other health services, as described in a County health care plan, as periodically amended, by levying a one quarter of one percent sales surtax for five years subject to automatic repeal if a 1% infrastructure surtax is levied?

Broward County, Question 2

Alternative Process for County Plat Approval

Currently under the Broward County Charter, all plats in Broward County must be approved by the County Commission prior to recording; this proposal amends the Charter to allow the County Commission to provide, by ordinance, as permitted under state law, an alternative process for approving plats, such as administrative approval, which might enable more expeditious plat approval including by allowing approval when the County Commission is in recess.

Revised Process for Selecting the County Auditor

Currently under the Broward County Charter, an independent board nominates one individual for County Auditor, and the County Commission ratifies that selection upon determining the nominee is qualified. Should the Charter be amended to provide that the independent board nominates three individuals, thereby enabling the County Commission to appoint as County Auditor the qualified nominee it determines is best able to serve in that position?

Deerfield Beach

City of Deerfield Beach - Question A

Clarification and Update of City Charter

Shall the City Charter be amended for style, clarity and consistency, along with amendments removing outdated provisions, and amendments updating the Charter to conform with State law provisions?

City of Deerfield Beach - Question B

Description of City Corporate Limits Update

The Charter includes a lengthy and outdated legal description of the corporate limits of the City. Shall the Charter be amended to replace the legal description with a map generally depicting the City's present official corporate limits, and provide that the official map of the corporate limits of the City shall be maintained by the office of the City Clerk?

City of Deerfield Beach - Question C

Two Year Residency Requirement for Candidates for Mayor or City Commissioner

The City Charter requires candidates for Mayor to have resided continuously in the City, and candidates for Commissioner to have resided continuously in their qualifying District, for six months immediately before qualifying. Shall the Charter be amended to instead require two years of continuous residency in the City immediately before qualifying for candidates for Mayor, and two years of continuous residency in the qualifying District for Commissioner candidates, except where non-residency results solely from redistricting?

City of Deerfield Beach - Question D

Change Municipal Election Dates to November of Even-Numbered Years and Transition of Commission Terms

Shall the Charter be amended to change Commission elections from March of odd-numbered years to November of even-numbered years beginning November 2028 to increase voter participation and decrease election costs, resulting in term extensions of approximately 20 months for all current Commissioners and the Mayor to maintain staggered terms, with no elections held in March 2027 or March 2029 and all future terms remaining four years?

City of Deerfield Beach - Question E

Removal of Referendum Election Requirement for the Sale, Transfer, or Lease of City-Owned Property

Shall the Charter be amended to remove the requirement that voters approve at a referendum election, and instead authorize the City Commission to approve, the sale, gift, trade, transfer, or lease of all or a portion of, or an interest in, city-owned real property with a fair market value exceeding $750,000, thereby eliminating the additional time and cost of conducting referendum elections for such transactions?

City of Deerfield Beach - Question F

Charter Review Board to Convene Every Ten Years

Shall the Charter be amended to require a Charter Review Board to be appointed by the City Commission in May 2035 and every ten years thereafter, with the Charter Review Board consisting of five regular voting members and five alternate members appointed by the City Commission to review the Charter and make non-binding recommendations to the Commission regarding amendments to the Charter?

City of Deerfield Beach - Question G

Amending Charter Section 11.01 Beach Area Development Regulations

Charter Section 11.01 restricts development east of the Intracoastal Waterway, including height limits of 45 feet for commercial and 55 feet for multifamily zoned properties, floor area ratio and lot coverage limits for such properties, mixed use and accessory use restrictions, and restrictions prohibiting rezoning to commercial. Shall the Charter be amended to remove only the floor area ratio regulations and the restrictions on mixed and accessory uses from the Charter?

City of Deerfield Beach - Question H

Amending Charter Section 11.02 Regarding Main Beach Parking Area Restrictions

Charter Section 11.02 restricts development on the City's main beach parking areas, including limiting permitted uses, restricting garage and parking space size, lot coverage, floor area ratio, and setbacks. Shall this Section be amended to remove the minimum parking space size requirements, the restriction on commercial use exceeding fifteen days annually, and the prohibition on use of this parking to satisfy other properties parking requirements?

City of Deerfield Beach - Question I

Four-Fifths Vote for Transition of Police or Fire Services

Shall the Charter be amended to require that any future action by the City Commission to transition, transfer, contract out, consolidate, or otherwise assign, police or fire services from the existing provider to another governmental entity or agency, special district, or sheriff's office, be approved by an affirmative vote of at least four-fifths (4/5) of the City Commission?

City of North Lauderdale - Question 4

PROHIBITING WAIVER OF REQUIREMENTS THAT THE CITY MANAGER MUST RESIDE IN BROWARD COUNTY, FLORIDA

The City Charter currently allows the City Commission to waive or excuse the requirement that the person serving as City Manager must reside in Broward County, Florida within six (6) months after being hired. It is proposed that the Charter be amended to prohibit the City Commission from waiving or excusing the requirement that the City Manager must reside in Broward County, Florida. Shall the above described Charter amendment be adopted?

City of North Lauderdale - Question 5

REVISING THE CHARTER REVIEW COMMISSION PROVISION

It is proposed that the Charter Review Commission be renamed the Charter Review Board, be expanded from 5 to 11 members, that the Board chair and vice-chair be designated by the City Commission, that State law be referenced for review process, and that the Board have access to convenient meeting places and equipment to enhance public participation in the Charter review process. Shall the above described Charter amendment be adopted?

CHANGING DATE OF COMMISSION ELECTION, EXTENDING CURRENT TERMS, SELECTION OF VICE-MAYOR

The current City Charter provides the mayor and commissioners with four-year terms, City elections in March of even numbered years, and the selection of the vice-mayor each April. Should the Charter be amended to change the election of the mayor and commissioners to November in even numbered years, with current terms extended 8 months to effectuate such changes as required by law, and to change the selection of vice-mayor to each December?
